Sound Profiles – Privacy Policy

App: Sound Profiles (com.blogspot.mravki.soundprofilestoggle) · Developer: Mincho Kolev

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Summary

Sound Profiles does not require an account and the developer does not collect, store or sell your personal data. Your profiles, schedules and settings stay on your device. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Menu → Privacy settings.

1. Data stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer:

  • Sound profiles (names, icons, volume levels, ringer mode, Do Not Disturb mode, brightness and vibration settings).
  • Schedules (days and times when a profile is applied automatically).
  • App and widget settings.
  • A short diagnostic log of automatic profile switches (time, profile name and result). It helps troubleshooting and is shown only inside the app; it is never uploaded.

Android backup. If you have enabled device backup in your Android settings, Android may include this data in your backup to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This is managed by Google and your device settings, not by the developer.

Export. When you choose to export your profiles, the exported text is shared only with the app or destination you select (for example email, a messenger or the clipboard).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Information about your device, operating system and the app;
  • Interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic/performance data.

Consent. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested. You can review or withdraw your consent at any time through Menu → Privacy settings in the app. Depending on your choice you will see personalized or non-personalized ads, or limited ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Other network connections

  • App configuration and promotions of the developer's other apps. The app downloads a small configuration file and promotional images from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). No personal data is sent; as with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Play in-app updates. The app uses Google Play services to check whether a newer version is available. This is handled by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Battery optimization help. If you open the help screen about background restrictions, the app loads device-specific instructions from dontkillmyapp.com. This request contains your device manufacturer, but no personal data.

4. Installed apps

The app checks whether specific apps published by the same developer are already installed, so that it does not promote apps you already have. Only this fixed list of apps is checked, the result is used only on your device and it is never sent anywhere.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by deleting profiles and schedules in the app, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Data processed by Google for advertising is subject to Google's retention policies and controls listed above.

8. Your rights

Because the developer does not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data held by the developer to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google, you can use the consent options in the app and the controls in your Google account and Android settings. If you have any question about your privacy, contact the developer at the address below.

9. Security

Your profiles and schedules are stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
