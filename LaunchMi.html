Privacy Policy

LaunchMi

In short: I do not collect, store or share your personal data. Your home screen, settings and apps stay on your device. The only data that leaves it is what Google AdMob uses for the ad on the settings screen.

1. Introduction

Welcome to LaunchMi ("the App"), a home screen (launcher) for Android, developed by Mincho Kolev ("I", "me", "my"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

2. Information I collect

I do NOT collect personal data. The App needs no account, email address, name or any other personal information, and it has no servers of its own that receive your data.

The App keeps the following only on your device:

  • Your home screen layout: pages, dock, folders, widgets and shortcuts
  • Your settings, hidden apps and the order of the long-press menus
  • The background picture you choose, copied into the App's private storage
  • The settings history, saved before an import or reset

This data stays on your device and is never sent to me or to third parties. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

3. The list of your apps

As a launcher, the App reads the list of apps installed on your device, with their names, icons and shortcuts, so it can show them on the home screen and in the app drawer. This list is used only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

4. Accessibility service

The App includes an optional Accessibility service, "LaunchMi screen lock". It is used only to:

  • Lock the screen when you double tap an empty spot on the home screen
  • Open the notification shade when you swipe down on the home screen

The service does not read the content of the screen, does not see what you type and does not collect, store or send any data. It is off until you turn it on yourself in Android's Accessibility settings, and you can turn it off there at any time.

5. Notification access

For the optional notification badges, the App can use Notification access, "LaunchMi badges". It only counts how many notifications each app has, to show that number on the app's icon. The App does not read, store or send the content of your notifications. Notification access is off until you allow it in Android's settings, and you can withdraw it at any time.

6. Export, import and sharing of settings

You can export your settings, hidden apps and home screen layout to a file. The file is saved where you choose, or sent to the app you pick when you use "Share settings". The App never sends this file anywhere on its own.

7. Ads

The App shows a banner ad only on its settings screen, never on your home screen. The ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain information to show and measure ads, including:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information (model, operating system)
  • Approximate location (based on the IP address)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with its own privacy law, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy options in the App.

When no AdMob ad is available, the App may show an ad for one of my other apps instead. For this it downloads a list of my apps and their images from GitHub. No information about you is sent with this request beyond what any web request contains (such as your IP address).

You can also limit personalized ads in your device settings or at Google Ad Settings. For more about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Third-party services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob - to show ads on the settings screen
  • Google User Messaging Platform - to ask for ad consent where the law requires it
  • Google Play - for in-app updates
  • GitHub - hosts the list of my other apps shown in place of AdMob ads

Each of these services has its own privacy policy, which I recommend reading.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or another age set by the applicable law), and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Data security

Since I do not collect or transfer personal data, the risk to your data is minimal. Everything the App stores is kept in its private storage on your device and protected by the security of the Android operating system.

11. Your rights

Under the applicable data protection laws, you can:

  • Delete all data of the App by uninstalling it or by clearing its data in your device settings
  • Turn off the Accessibility service and Notification access at any time in Android's settings
  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy options in the App
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device settings

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

13. Consent

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
